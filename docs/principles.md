# Principles

Flashcard Reels brings the low-friction feel of social media feeds to serious study. These
principles hold across every phase. Requirements and specs follow from them, and a feature that
breaks one needs a strong reason. Each principle names what you can observe when it holds.

1. **Low friction.** The app reuses gestures people already know from social media feeds, and
   points them at learning.
   _You can see it when_ every destination is reachable by swiping, and studying needs only
   swipe, double-tap, hold, and tap.
2. **Hook first.** Opening the app shows a card right away.
   _You can see it when_ a cold launch shows a card with zero taps, even on a fresh install.
3. **Think before seeing.** The answer stays hidden until the learner asks for it.
   _You can see it when_ no screen in the feeds shows an answer before a double-tap.
4. **Learn, then recall.** Lessons let the learner meet new material before being tested on it.
   _You can see it when_ every card of a daily deck links to a lesson section, and one tap from
   the card opens it.
5. **Nothing is forced.** The learner moves freely between the feeds, Focus, and lessons.
   _You can see it when_ no feature asks the learner to finish one step before another is
   available.
6. **Local-first.** Studying needs no account, network, or portal.
   _You can see it when_ the app studies, reads, backs up, and restores in airplane mode, and
   the only network request it makes is a QR deck download.
7. **Progress is never lost.** Learning data outlives deck content and is keyed by stable IDs.
   _You can see it when_ updating the app, updating or removing a deck, and restoring a backup
   leave review counts unchanged unless the learner chose a reset or start fresh.
